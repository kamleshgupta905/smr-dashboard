### Summary of Daily Operations:
1. **Login**: URL `https://smr.vmhgroupsmr.com/` par `vmhgroup` / `Jelly` se sign-in karein.
2. **Sync**: Header me blue **"Sync"** button dabayein taaki latest active inventory aa jaye.
3. **Search & Filter**: Surveys page par country aur CPI ($5+) choose karein.
4. **Allotment**:
   - Single survey ke liye: Row me **"Assign"** dabayein ➔ Vendor choose karein ➔ Link copy karein.
   - 10-15 surveys ke liye: Checkboxes tick karein ➔ Upar **"Bulk Assign to Vendor"** dabayein ➔ 1 click me assign karke Excel CSV download karein.
5. **Vendor Management**: Supply Partners me vendor search karein, RevShare 60% karke **Save** dabayein (permanently save rahega).
6. **Reports**: Reports Hub par 1 click karein ➔ Consolidated overview aur **"View Details"** se profit margin dekhein.
7. **Accounts & Payout**: Payments tab se **"Export Bank Batch CSV"** download karke accounts team ko bhejein.
